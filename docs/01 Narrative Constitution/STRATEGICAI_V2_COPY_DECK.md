# StrategicAI v2 Public Copy Deck

## Canonical narrative spine

Everyone has a piece. You are still putting the pieces together. StrategicAI
brings available evidence into a shared operating picture. People closest to
the work correct it. Nemo helps you interrogate and reason from it. Then
accountable leaders decide what deserves action.

Operational Reality is the business itself. The shared operating picture is a
representation of it. Operational Intelligence is usable understanding formed
from that picture.

## Executive outcome layer

Use the following as governed value framing, not guarantees or customer proof:

| Outcome | Permitted public framing |
| --- | --- |
| Executive visibility | See what is actually happening across the business without reconstructing it from meetings, dashboards, messages, and memory. |
| Earlier risk detection | Notice dependencies, exceptions, and weak handoffs before they become expensive surprises. |
| Board preparation | Enter important conversations with a clearer picture of what changed, what is uncertain, and where attention belongs. |
| Leadership alignment | Give different functions a place to compare what they believe is happening and correct the picture together. |
| Trusted answers | Ask better questions from company context, with unknowns and disagreement still visible. |
| Decision velocity | Spend less time rebuilding the story and more time deciding what deserves action. |

The homepage may present this as a scannable executive-outcomes section. The
product page should connect each product component to one outcome without
turning the connection into a guarantee.

## Page jobs and copy direction

### `/` — Why should I care?

Lead with recognition: people, systems, files, conversations, and memory each
hold a piece while the owner is still assembling the story.

Use:

> Your people, systems, files, meetings, and memory each hold part of the
> answer. StrategicAI brings the available evidence into a shared operating
> picture that people can inspect, challenge, and correct.

Also make explicit:

> Your systems don't contain everything your business knows.

Retain:

> What becomes possible when the full picture no longer lives in one head?

Use “full picture” only as the owner-burden transition. Do not use it as a
capability or completeness claim. Close toward See Founding 100.

### `/how-it-works` — What happens to messy information?

Own the process sequence:

> Evidence → formation → disagreement and uncertainty → correction → shared
> operating picture → warranted action

Required language:

> Different answers are not noise. They are part of the picture.

> What nobody knows yet belongs in the picture too.

> A useful picture does not require false agreement.

Use one clearly labelled illustrative handoff example across Evidence, Picture,
Correction, and Ask Nemo. It is not a customer result.

### `/product` — What are the parts?

Own the product sequence:

> Operational Reality → Operational Intelligence → Business Views → Nemo →
> Capabilities

Define each term without duplicating the process page. State that Operational
Intelligence begins with available evidence and correction, not with a
dashboard, blank prompt, or automation.

For each component, answer both “what is it?” and “what executive outcome does
it help create?” Use bounded connections: visibility, earlier risk
recognition, board preparation, leadership alignment, trusted answers, and
decision velocity are intended value framing, not guaranteed results.

For Nemo, use the sharper distinction sparingly:

> Most AI begins with your question. Nemo begins with the context behind it.

### `/pricing` — Ways to Work

Use the visible label **Ways to Work** while preserving `/pricing`.

> Founding 100 is the current public place to begin.

> Start by building enough shared understanding of the business to decide what
> deserves attention next.

> Current participation details and approved commercial terms are available on
> the Founding 100 offer page.

Describe what may come later without reinstating the old Executive Brief,
Diagnostic, Roadmap, Implementation, or Execution ladder.

### `/partners` — How do I bring better context into implementation?

Use:

> Bring better context into the work before implementation begins.

> Your client already has pieces of the operating story spread across people,
> systems, decisions, history, and exceptions. StrategicAI helps turn those
> pieces into a shared operating picture that the people closest to the work
> can inspect and correct before consequential change begins.

Govern with:

> Evidence before prescription. Authority before action. Context before
> automation. Claims only where the evidence supports them.

Primary CTA: **Experience StrategicAI through Founding 100**.

### `/founding100/quick` — Why does the same operating problem keep returning?

Use only this arc:

> Repeating problem → fragmented context → everyone has a piece → build the
> picture → correct it → ask a better question

Close with:

> Bring one recurring problem. Start there.

### `/founding100/webinar` — Why must understanding precede intervention?

Teach fragmented context, the contents of an operating picture, disagreement,
correction, Business Views, Nemo, warranted action, and Founding 100 in that
order.

### `/founding100/offer` — What happens if I say yes?

Retain the recognition-led headline:

> If everyone has a piece and you’re still the one putting it together, start
> here.

Add **Bring the messy version** and **A good place to start** qualification.
Keep all approved price, checkout, cohort, entitlement, and 90-day claims here
only. Add data-trust copy only after policy and runtime approval.

### `/founding100` — What do I do now?

Keep:

> You’re in.

Then:

> Start with one real problem.

> Don’t clean it up first.

> You do not need the whole company mapped before you begin.

This is an onboarding route, not an acquisition page, and is `noindex`.

### `/intake` and `/intake/thanks`

Use the intake to teach the epistemology through field microcopy. Use the
confirmation to reinforce that each perspective is one piece of the picture.
Preserve form, auth, storage, endpoint, redirect, and analytics behavior.

## Banned active expressions

Do not publish “Build My Executive Brief,” “AI Brain,” “TrustConsole,”
“Business Intelligence Portfolio,” or the former Executive Brief → Diagnostic
→ Roadmap → Implementation architecture on active v2 surfaces.

“Executive Brief” may remain only where it is the actual backend artifact or a
historical source reference.

## Trust contract

Where the runtime supports the behavior, use:

> Built to be challenged, not blindly trusted.

The safe public pillars are: different perspectives can remain different,
humans correct the picture, unknowns remain visible, and people remain
accountable for action. Do not add provenance, security, retention, training
use, auditability, access-control, or integration claims without approved
policy and runtime evidence.
