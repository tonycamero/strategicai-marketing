# StrategicAI v2.1 Public Narrative Diff

This record describes the bounded v2.1 copy pass. It changes narrative and
commercial doctrine only. It does not create runtime behavior, persistence,
integrations, entitlements, product contracts, or new commercial commitments.

## `/`

**KEEP**

- “Why does the full picture of how your business works still live in your
  head?”
- Golden Proof Thread and the rhetorical Founding 100 close.

**STRENGTHEN**

- Hero support: “StrategicAI brings the available evidence into a shared,
  evolving operating picture that people can inspect, challenge, and
  correct—and frontier intelligence can reason against.”
- Consequence: “One business. No shared picture.” followed by management drag.
- AI distinction: “Frontier models provide intelligence. StrategicAI provides
  organizational reality.”
- Executive bridge from shared context to better questions, trustworthy
  answers, earlier visibility, alignment, and decisions.

**SIMPLIFY**

- Keep Nemo questions selective and concrete rather than presenting a feature
  matrix.

**REMOVE OR DE-EMPHASIZE**

- Blank-chat framing as the whole AI problem.
- Outcome cards that read as detached benefit claims.

**ADD**

- Incomplete, inconsistent, transient, and stale context as the deeper AI
  grounding problem.
- Shared-organizational correction and bounded questions across time.

## `/how-it-works`

**KEEP** evidence, formation, disagreement, correction, the illustrative
handoff, and warranted action.

**STRENGTHEN** the picture as context multiple people can inspect, dispute, and
correct together.

**ADD** a bounded change-and-continuity step using “What changed?”, “What does
that change affect?”, and “What now deserves attention?”

**REMOVE OR DE-EMPHASIZE** any inference of automatic monitoring, automatic
updating, or guaranteed risk detection.

## `/product`

**KEEP** Operational Reality → Operational Intelligence → Business Views →
Nemo → capabilities.

**STRENGTHEN** the primary distinction: a persistent, inspectable, correctable
representation of operating reality gives frontier intelligence business
context to reason against.

**ADD** the plain-language distinction between frontier intelligence and
organizational reality, with an adjacent qualification that the picture is an
evidence-bounded representation.

**SIMPLIFY** Nemo as the reasoning interface rather than the durable asset.

**REMOVE OR DE-EMPHASIZE** model choice, BYOM, automatic updating, and automatic
notification as current product promises.

## `/pricing`

**KEEP** Ways to Work, Founding 100 as the current public entry, and separate
scope for later work.

**STRENGTHEN** only through adjacent site context. Do not duplicate the
intelligence doctrine or 90-day compounding language here.

## `/partners`

**KEEP** better context before implementation and the existing governance
principles.

**ADD** the management-drag consequence: shared context can reduce repeated
discovery, reconciliation, and reliance on the owner as organizational
historian.

## `/founding100/quick`

**KEEP** one recurring problem as the entire short-form arc.

**STRENGTHEN** the explanation that fragmented context consumes leadership
attention through repeated reconstruction.

**REMOVE OR DE-EMPHASIZE** the full product architecture and automation.

## `/founding100/webinar`

**KEEP** the deeper worldview role.

**ADD** management drag, shared organizational context, change over time, and
Nemo as grounded reasoning before warranted action.

## `/founding100/offer`

**KEEP** the recognition-led headline, current approved terms, Bring the messy
version, qualification, Business Views, inspection and correction, Nemo, and
the existing scope boundary.

**STRENGTHEN** the value distinction:

> Frontier models provide intelligence. StrategicAI provides organizational
> reality.

**ADD** gated compounding-value framing:

> Over the 90-day Founding 100 experience, Formation, Business Views,
> available evidence, corrections, Nemo interactions, and changes over time
> can add context to the business picture.

> The value is not only in the first picture. It can grow as people inspect
> it, correct it, return with better questions, and add what has changed.

**QUALIFY** the accumulation as an intended consequence of the existing
experience—not a separate artifact, named deliverable, completeness claim,
retention promise, or automatic-update capability.

## `/founding100`, `/intake`, `/intake/thanks`, and `/login`

artifact boundaries, and noindex state.

**REMOVE OR DE-EMPHASIZE** acquisition doctrine, price, and offer claims from
these routes.

## Shared chrome and metadata

grounded-intelligence distinction.

**KEEP** canonical CTAs, unique route metadata, sitemap membership, noindex
