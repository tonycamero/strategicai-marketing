# PUB-001 StrategicAI Public Website v2

## Publication job

Help an accountable owner, operator, or partner recognize the cost of
fragmented operating context, understand the evidence-to-picture-to-question
method, understand the intended executive outcomes of that sequence, and choose
whether to review the Founding 100 offer.

## Publication boundaries

The website may state ratified product doctrine and controlled market language.
Runtime behavior, customer outcomes, and commercial commitments remain bounded
by current receipts and approval records. The v2 website must not imply a
complete or omniscient business model, autonomous authority, a guaranteed
outcome, or an approved offer where the commercial gate is not open.

| Intake | Participation | Preserve backend and auth contracts | Begin the shared picture |

## Release blockers

- Constitutional amendment approval or explicit exception.
- Claim register review for every material statement.
- Commercial approval for price, checkout, cohort, entitlement, and 90-day
  language.
- Policy and runtime authority before publishing data-trust answers.
- Executive outcomes remain explicitly framed as intended value connections,
  not guaranteed results or customer outcomes.
- Unique raw HTML title, description, canonical, OG, Twitter, and robots state
  for every route.
- Build, route, copy, static-render, regression, and accessibility checks pass.

## Indexing and search cleanup

The sitemap contains only externally indexable routes: `/`, `/how-it-works`,
`/product`, `/pricing`, `/partners`, `/founding100/quick`,
`/founding100/webinar`, and `/founding100/offer`. Post-purchase, intake,
authentication, aliases, and redirect-only routes are excluded and carry
`noindex` where they render HTML.

After deployment, verify deployed raw HTML, canonical URLs, sitemap contents,
and stale indexed language through the available search-console workflow.
