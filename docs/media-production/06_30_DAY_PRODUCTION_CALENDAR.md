## M. Operating assumptions

- Day 1 is Monday, August 24, 2026.
- The founder has one concentrated Wave 1 shoot block and one smaller matched-conflict block.
- A producer/editor handles asset preparation between founder review windows.
- The `SEE` transport, both ungated content routes/assets, and market-learning measurement chain can be cleared independently of the offer. If they are not, film may continue, but publishing moves until the gates applicable to the exact asset are cleared.
- Paid promotion is not required during the first organic read. Any paid test receives a separate budget, post instance ID, and constant policy.
- StrategicAI MVP work remains protected by timeboxing founder media work and avoiding daily filming.

## Tier 2 activation branch

If a Tier 2 pain qualifies by Day 24:

1. Choose one concept with the exact market language that activated it.
2. Do not replace a matched B02 conflict cell; place the Tier 2 test in B03 or replace a lower-learning B03 candidate.
3. Mark it `TIER2-DISCOVERY`; do not compare it directly with a mature winner expansion.
4. Publish once to the channel where the signal originated and once to a second plausible channel.
5. Hold the remaining Tier 2 concept until the first receives a seven-day read.

If no Tier 2 pain qualifies, do nothing. The backlog is functioning correctly by remaining a backlog.

## Paid promotion gate after Day 30

Paid promotion may begin only when:

- organic or outbound-assisted evidence identifies at least one qualified pain state;
- tracking reconciles `SEE` to DM delivery and content; offer and checkout
  tracking is added only when that branch is authorized;
- the short/deep content produces credible qualified behavior; any offer has
  separately passed its commercial conversion and delivery-readiness review;
- G-01 through G-09 are closed as applicable;
- delivery capacity has a written pause threshold;
- the paid test preserves clip/edit/CTA identity and does not introduce stronger claims.

Start with a small, bounded test across the best organic post and one controlled variation. Do not scale spend to solve broken transport, content, offer, checkout, follow-up, or delivery capacity.
