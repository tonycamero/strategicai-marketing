# Founding 100 Belief / Objection / Proof Matrix

## Document contract

This artifact translates the sales-sequencing architecture in `15` into the
dependency structure required before designing a 35-minute conversion
presentation. It is a crosswalk, not another descriptive inventory.

It does not own product doctrine, commercial terms, runtime capability, offer
activation, page implementation, or final narration.

```text
PRODUCT_DOCTRINE != MARKET_LANGUAGE != RUNTIME_CAPABILITY != COMMERCIAL_COMMITMENT
FINAL_NARRATION = AUTHORIZED_FOR_DRAFT
35_MINUTE_BEAT_SHEET = AUTHORIZED_AS_DRAFT
APPLICATION_SOURCE_CHANGES = 0
ASSUME_LAUNCH_STATE_PROOF = TRUE
P0_PROOF_RECEIPTS = REQUIRED_BEFORE_PUBLICATION / NOT_REQUIRED_BEFORE_NARRATION
FINAL_PUBLICATION = BLOCKED_UNTIL_PROOF_AND_COMMERCIAL_ACCEPTANCE
```

The crosswalk asks: **what has to happen for one belief to become strong enough
to support the next?** A belief threshold is not proof that a capability exists.
Conversion behavior can promote, retest, demote, or retire market language; it
cannot promote it into runtime or commercial truth. Narration drafting is now
authorized against the launch-state proof assumption; publication still
requires accepted proof receipts and commercial acceptance.

## Stage key

The stages are presentation jobs, not minute assignments. A later beat sheet
may compress, reorder, or run some jobs in parallel after this matrix is
reviewed.

| Stage | Job |
| --- | --- |
| S01 RECOGNITION | “This is happening in my company.” |
| S02 TENSION | The current explanation is no longer comfortable. |
| S03 CONSEQUENCE | Doing nothing has visible non-dollar costs. |
| S04 INCOMPLETE EXPLANATIONS | Common fixes may solve a local symptom while leaving the operating picture missing. |
| S05 NEW EXPLANATION | The object to understand is how the business actually works. |
| S06 NEW PARADIGM | Operational Reality can become Operational Intelligence. |
| S07 UNIQUE MECHANISM | Evidence, participation, correction, and bounded views precede intervention. |
| S08 DEMONSTRATION / PROOF | Show only what the evidence supports. |
| S09 OBJECTION REVERSAL | Treat resistance as a signal and resolve the underlying concern. |
| S10 DESIRED FUTURE | The owner can see, question, and correct the company picture. |
| S11 OFFER TRANSITION | Connect the desired future to the authorized Founding 100 experience. |
| S12 VALUE BUILD | Make the value chain legible before price. |
| S13 PRICE / TERMS | State only an approved scope, entitlement, and terms. |
| S14 WHY FOUNDING 100 / WHY NOW | Use only a real authorized cohort, capacity, date, or decision boundary. |
| S15 RISK / UNCERTAINTY | Make inspection, correction, support, and unresolved boundaries explicit. |
| S16 DECISION | Make the authorized next action clear, or use the qualified fallback. |

## Belief crosswalk

The 15 beliefs below are the existing `B01–B15` inventory from `15`, now
crosswalked to prerequisites, blockers, proof burden, presentation placement,
and observable threshold signals. `PROOF_AVAILABLE_NOW` records repository
evidence as of this document; the beat sheet is designed against the launch
state in which the explicitly named product proofs are accepted and
demonstrable. Pre-publication release review must verify each assumed proof
asset. Customer outcome proof remains separately prohibited without consented
evidence.

## Context-rich AI and Ask Nemo expansion

This is an experiential overlay on the existing belief inventory, not a new
category or a new product claim. B06, B10, and B13 should be read as one
progression:

```text
Useful AI reasoning is shaped by the context behind the question.
    → business context is fragmented across people, systems, history, and work
    → repeatedly explaining fragments is not durable Operational Intelligence
    → relevant company context lets Nemo begin closer to the consequential question
    → the owner can see more clearly and can Ask Nemo better questions
    → the first 100 help discover the real value through use and correction
```

The behavioral phrase is **Ask Nemo**. It means “ask from the understanding the
company has already built,” not “ask an omniscient assistant.” It does not add
authority to Nemo or guarantee that the answer is complete, correct, or
actionable.

### SEE → ASK acceptance test

The deep experience passes this overlay when a prospect can say both:

- “I understand what I might see about how my business actually works.”
- “I already know what I would Ask Nemo first.”

The offer passes when the buyer understands that the value is not generic AI
access. It is a company-specific understanding plus the authorized experience
of bringing real questions to Nemo from that context.

## Offer-readiness gate

This gate concerns readiness to hear the offer transition. It is not commercial
authorization and does not substitute for G-01, G-07, or G-09.

```text
OFFER_READINESS_GATE = PASS CONDITIONS
```

Pass requires that the prospect can, in their own language or through a
qualified conversation:

- identify a recurring operating condition rather than only a one-off symptom;
- name at least one non-dollar consequence of leaving it unexamined;
- understand why a document, dashboard, SaaS tool, generic AI, agent, agency,
  consultant, meeting, or hire may solve a local problem without inherently
  creating durable, inspectable, correctable understanding;
- understand “how the business actually works” as the plain-language object of
  inquiry;
- distinguish Operational Reality from a complete/static/omniscient model;
- understand Operational Intelligence as the resulting decision-relevant
  understanding, not a document pile;
- understand that understanding can precede automation, while automation
  remains a possible downstream intervention;
- understand that perspectives, disagreement, unknowns, and correction matter;
- understand the intended role of Business Views as bounded projections;
- understand Nemo as intended to reason from accepted organizational context,
  without assuming guaranteed current or 90-day continuity;
- see a truthful mechanism proof or process explanation sufficient to make the
  category intelligible, with any missing runtime proof named honestly;
- feel a desired owner state: seeing dependencies, questions, unknowns, and
  priorities more clearly;
- have O01, O03, O05, O06, O07, O08, O09, and O12 reduced enough to continue;
  unresolved scope/price/timing objections belong after the transition; and
- be able to state the next question they want to investigate, or accept a
  qualified next step to determine fit.

The gate fails if the prospect believes StrategicAI already understands the
whole company, expects a guaranteed Portfolio, assumes Nemo has guaranteed
context, requires a promised outcome, or is being moved by fabricated urgency.

The gate can authorize a future offer transition only when the commercial branch
is separately authorized. When it is inactive, PASS means the prospect is ready
for a qualified conversation or continued content—not a purchase CTA.
