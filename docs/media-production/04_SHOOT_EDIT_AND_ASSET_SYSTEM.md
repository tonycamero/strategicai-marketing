# Shoot, Edit, and Asset System

## F. Founder shoot plan

### Production objective

Capture eight approved pain-state clips plus hooks, CTAs, and reusable neutral B-roll in one four-to-five-hour B01 production block. Capture the matched C1–C4 Direct Attack Test in the existing 90-minute B02 block if G-04 review passes. Keep the softer/general Category Contrast slate in backlog. The visual standard is credible operator, not studio advertisement.

### Category Contrast capture decision

```text
CATEGORY_CONTRAST_CAPTURE = B02
B02_SLATE = C1_C2_C3_C4_MATCHED_CONFLICT_TEST
GENERAL_CATEGORY_CONTRAST_CAPTURE = DEFER
```

B01 is already full. Adding four complete clips would increase the founder’s
planned four-to-five-hour day and weaken delivery across the original eight.
B02 already contains a 90-minute founder window and a maximum-four-asset cap.
The matched C1–C4 Direct Attack Test occupies those four slots. The general
CC01–CC04 slate remains backlog, and pain-state winner expansion moves to B03
or the next authorized batch. Do not add another slate to B02 or silently
extend the founder time budget.

Category Contrast delivery should feel:

- calm;
- informed;
- certain;
- skeptical or slightly incredulous;
- amused when natural;
- capable of direct category attack without manic energy, shouting, contempt, or personal hostility.

Founder posture:

> **I cannot believe everyone is skipping this obvious step.**

AI automation agencies may be named and criticized as a category. Do not use
named companies or individual creators, screenshots or stitches targeting a
specific agency, personal mockery, fake agent demonstrations, or a “guru
reacts” format without separate evidence and authority.

### Direct Attack Test capture decision

```text
DIRECT_ATTACK_CAPTURE = B02
```

For B02, hold the setting, wardrobe,
camera, body structure, CTA, and approximate length constant. Change the hook
only. Use `PLAIN_FIRST_WITH_NAMED_BRIDGE` for all four: “how the business
actually works” leads, and “Operational Reality” appears as the name for that
object. Do not combine the conflict-intensity test with a second naming
variable.
Record in ascending order from C1 to C4, then capture all four hooks once more
in reverse order to control for warm-up and fatigue.

Performance direction for C3 and C4: lower the voice, hold eye contact, and let
the accusation land. Do not shout, sneer, imitate a competitor, or perform
anger. A quiet attack should feel more dangerous than theatrical outrage.

### Minimum crew

| Role | Minimum responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Founder/talent | Own the thought, deliver naturally, approve usable meaning |
| Producer/script supervisor | Clip ID, claim wording, take log, continuity, time control |
| Camera/audio operator | Framing, exposure, focus, sound, media cards, backups |
| Editor, optional on set | Checks edit points, caption-safe framing, and pickup needs |

Two people can cover the shoot if producer and camera roles are combined. The founder should not manage files or troubleshoot audio while performing.

### Batch map by location

| Block | Location | Clips | Visual logic | Planned time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | Founder desk / ordinary office | HR, HIRE, TSB | Decisions, staffing, and fragmented information feel natural here | 75 minutes |
| L2 | Whiteboard / workshop / neutral operational wall | CIMH, BH, LP | Memory, handoffs, and visibility benefit from physical space and simple marks | 80 minutes |
| L3 | Walking exterior or parked vehicle | FF, VAC | End-of-day interruption and inability to leave feel lived, not staged | 60 minutes |
| L4 | Neutral B-roll circuit | All | Phones, notes, handoffs, generic screens, empty chair, calendar, walking | 35 minutes |
| Buffer | Any | Pickups and failures | Hooks, CTA variants, room tone, technical retakes | 30 minutes |

Do not film while driving. Remove client names, screens, license plates, addresses, employee faces, and confidential operational details.

### Grouping by emotional delivery

Use this order inside each block to protect the founder’s energy:

1. **Calm provocation:** HR, TSB, HIRE.
2. **Reflective consequence:** CIMH, LP, VAC.
3. **Lived frustration:** FF, BH.

For B02 Direct Attack, preserve a single quiet-certain register across C1–C4.
Record the four complete clips in ascending order, then record the four hooks
again in reverse order. The softer `CC01`–`CC04` cards are not captured in this
batch.

The conflict level comes from the claim, not an escalating volume or anger
performance.

Record the most emotionally demanding take after one clean warm-up, not at the end of the day. Reset for two minutes between pain states so frustration from one clip does not bleed into the next.

### Wardrobe strategy

- Use three solid, operator-credible looks: one per location block.
- Prefer mid-tone blue, charcoal, olive, rust, or neutral overshirt. Avoid tiny patterns, bright white, pure black against dark walls, visible third-party logos, and “tech founder” costume signals.
- Keep glasses, watch, facial hair, and jacket state consistent within a clip.
- Photograph the finished look at the start of each block for pickups.
- A wardrobe change signals a different recording moment and makes repeated publishing feel less like one batch.

### Background strategy

- The background should contain two or three believable working cues, not clutter.
- Keep screens generic and out of focus. Never manufacture customer dashboards to imply current integrations.
- Whiteboard marks should be abstract boxes/arrows with no architecture jargon.
- Preserve depth: founder approximately 1.2–2 meters from the background when space allows.
- Avoid branded banners, LED walls, empty conference rooms, virtual backgrounds, and excessive RGB lighting.

### Capture standard

| Dimension | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Orientation | Native vertical `9:16` |
| Capture resolution | Prefer `4K 2160x3840` if the phone/camera supports stable recording; minimum `1080x1920` |
| Delivery resolution | `1080x1920` MP4, H.264, high-quality VBR |
| Frame rate | 30 fps; lock it for the whole batch |
| Shutter | Approximately 1/60 when manual controls are available |
| Exposure | Lock before each take; protect skin highlights |
| White balance | Lock per location; do not use shifting auto white balance |
| Focus | Eye detect or locked manual focus; test movement for L3 |
| Composition | Eyes near upper third; shoulders visible; text-safe space above/below |
| Safe area | Keep face and key text away from edges; reserve roughly top 14% and bottom 20% for platform UI/CTA risk |

### Audio

- Primary: wired or wireless lavalier placed consistently 15–20 cm below the mouth.
- Backup: camera-mounted shotgun or second recorder whenever available.
- Record 10 seconds of room tone at each location.
- Monitor with headphones during the first full take of every setup.
- Disable fans, notification sounds, and nearby machinery when possible.
- If the location sounds bad, move. Expensive visuals cannot rescue unclear speech.
- Record one hand clap after announcing the clip ID for sync, but trim every slate from the usable opening.

### Lighting

- Place soft window/key light 30–45 degrees from the founder.
- Add a simple reflector or low-output fill if the shadow side loses the eyes.
- Use practical background lights only when they already belong in the space.
- Check LED and overhead fixtures for flicker before all takes.
- Outdoor clips use open shade; avoid changing direct sun and squinting.
- Match skin tone across takes before leaving a location.

### Teleprompter and delivery method

Use a beat prompter, not a scrolling paragraph:

```text
EXACT HOOK
recognition examples
one reframe
StrategicAI possibility + correction boundary
EXACT CTA
```

- The exact hook and approved claim sentence may be displayed word-for-word.
- The middle should be delivered from four or five short meaning beats.
- Set the prompter close enough to the lens that eye movement is not visible.
- If the founder sounds read, remove the prompter for Take 2 and speak to one imagined owner.
- Memorization is not required. Understanding the turn in the argument is required.

### Advisor-line delivery

When a clip uses a line such as “What would you ask if your advisor already knew your business?” or “Nemo has somewhere real to start,” slow down enough for the owner to mentally answer. Hold a natural half-beat after the question before moving to the CTA. Do not rush the advisor proposition into a feature explanation.

- Look directly into the lens as if asking one owner, not presenting a category.
- Let the question breathe; one short silence can be more persuasive than another sentence.
- Keep the emotional register curious and grounded, never omniscient or therapeutic.
- If the line makes the founder sound like Nemo is a professional advisor, reshoot with “thinking partner” or “somewhere real to start.”
- Capture a clean question-only pickup for later testing, but keep it after recognition and reframe in the edit.

### Take structure

For every clip:

1. **Take 1 — control:** exact approved script at natural pace.
2. **Take 2 — conversational:** same claims, shorter sentences, speak to one owner.
3. **Take 3 — contrast:** 10% more energy at the hook, then slower reframe.
4. **Hook pickups:** exact hook once urgent, once quiet, once with a deliberate pause.
5. **Bridge pickup:** the StrategicAI sentence by itself.
6. **CTA pickups:** record all approved CTA variants below.
7. **Safety pickup:** repeat any line containing a stumble, accidental overclaim, client reference, or awkward edit point.

Do not chase a perfect 15th full take. If three takes fail, pause, shorten the beat card, and explain the idea conversationally before recording again.

### Standard CTA pickups

Record the default in every wardrobe/location. Record the declared wording
test only when the experiment manifest calls for it. Both use keyword `SEE`;
neither points directly to a webpage.

| CTA code | Line | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CTA1` | “Comment SEE and I’ll send you the video.” | Canonical default |
| `CTA2` | “Comment SEE and I’ll send you the walkthrough.” | Declared wording test; do not use inside hook-comparison cells unless held constant |

Legacy `CTA3`–`CTA6` direct-link pickups are retired. Do not record them for
new assets.

### B-roll capture list

Capture each shot for 6–8 seconds, both locked and with one slow movement:

- owner phone lighting up, face down and face up;
- hand moving a note between two columns;
- incomplete generic form or work order with all real data removed;
- calendar plan interrupted by sticky notes;
- empty owner chair;
- founder opening a laptop and switching among generic tabs;
- founder calling, waiting, and writing one answer in a notebook;
- two arrows on a whiteboard that do not connect;
- folder or packet passed between two people without faces;
- walking through a local business district;
- job description page set aside;
- generic “late,” “waiting,” and “needs approval” cards created for production.

B-roll supports a spoken moment. It must not imply a product screen, integration, customer result, or real client condition that has not been evidenced.

### Shoot-day quality gate

Before changing location, confirm:

- one technically clean full take per clip;
- one natural full take per clip;
- three clean hooks per clip;
- clean bridge and CTA pickups;
- no overclaim, client data, third-party logo, flicker, focus drift, or clipped audio;
- 10 seconds of room tone;
- producer logged the preferred take and backup.

### Raw footage management

Use this folder model:

```text
F100_MEDIA/
  00_ADMIN/
  01_RAW/
    B01_20260828/
      CAM_A/
      AUDIO_A/
      AUDIO_B/
  02_PROJECTS/
  03_MASTERS/
  04_PLATFORM_EXPORTS/
  05_CAPTIONS/
  06_THUMBNAILS/
  07_POST_COPY/
  08_REPORTING/
  99_ARCHIVE/
```

- Treat camera originals as immutable.
- Ingest to the primary drive and one separate backup before formatting a card or deleting anything.
- Compare file counts and total bytes after each copy; checksum if the transfer tool supports it.
- Keep project autosaves and fonts/logo assets with the project.
- Store releases/consents in `00_ADMIN`.
- Retain raw footage through at least the Founding 100 campaign plus 90 days, subject to the approved retention policy.
- Move superseded masters into `99_ARCHIVE`; do not overwrite the prior export.

## G. Editing system

### Repeatable edit timeline

| Time | Job | Edit behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.00–0.30s | Stop | Founder already in position; spoken hook and hook text begin immediately |
| 0.30–3.00s | Confirm pain | Stay on face unless one highly specific insert improves recognition |
| 3.00–15.00s | Build scene | Remove filler; use one or two B-roll inserts or a subtle punch-in |
| 15.00–27.00s | Reframe | Return to founder; slow slightly; display only the key distinction |
| 27.00–35.00s | Possibility + CTA | Name StrategicAI/Nemo after recognition; one CTA; optional 0.8s end treatment |
| 35.00–45.00s | Exception only | Allowed when natural delivery or clarity earns it; do not add feature explanation |

### Pace and cuts

- Target one visual change every 2–4 seconds, including purposeful punch-ins.
- Cut dead air longer than approximately 250 ms unless a pause creates tension.
- Preserve the founder’s breath before the reframe when it makes the line feel considered.
- Use jump cuts at sentence or clause boundaries; never in the middle of a key noun or claim.
- Limit digital punch-ins to two sizes: 100% and 112–118%.
- Do not add fast motion, whooshes, meme inserts, stock-office montages, or animated diagrams by default.
- Produce one restrained master before testing a higher-cut edit variation.

### First-frame requirements

The first frame must:

- contain the founder’s face, not a logo or black frame;
- be visually different from the surrounding feed through expression, environment, or movement;
- begin the exact spoken hook immediately;
- show a short hook overlay nearly verbatim;
- remain legible with sound off;
- contain no greeting, throat-clear, slate, animated intro, or slow push-in.

### Caption and subtitle format

- Burn accurate captions into every platform master; also export `.srt` where native upload supports it.
- Use sentence case, not all caps, for full subtitles.
- Maximum two lines at once; break on meaning, not arbitrary character count.
- Keep subtitles centered within the safe area and above platform controls.
- Use high-contrast white text with a dark box or shadow; one restrained accent may mark a key phrase.
- Do not caption filler words removed from the edit.
- Review proper nouns: `StrategicAI` and `Nemo`.
- Hook overlay may use all caps; subtitles should not.

### Hook text

- 3–7 words preferred; maximum 10.
- Keep it visible for 1.5–2.5 seconds.
- Place above the subtitle zone without covering eyes or mouth.
- Use the spoken meaning, not clickbait that the clip does not fulfill.

### B-roll policy

- Use B-roll only to clarify the exact moment being spoken.
- Limit most clips to zero, one, or two inserts.
- Keep the founder visible for the reframe and CTA.
- Never use fake customer screens, fabricated dashboards, AI-generated client operations, or generic robots.
- A talking-head-only edit is acceptable when delivery is strong.

### Branding and end treatment

- No logo animation at the opening.
- First brand mention normally occurs in the spoken possibility after the reframe.
- A small StrategicAI wordmark may appear during the final 3–5 seconds.
- End treatment: 0.6–1.0 seconds within the target duration, not an appended multi-second ad card.
- End text: `COMMENT SEE` plus the verified keyword-delivery promise.
- Do not display `$299` or “first 100” until commercial gate G-01 is approved and the test specifically calls for offer-forward copy.

### Music policy

- Default: no music. The founder’s voice and operating environment carry credibility.
- If a music variation is tested, use a licensed low-complexity instrumental with no lyrical competition.
- Keep music approximately 18–24 dB below speech and duck it further under the hook.
- Never make a trending track essential to meaning or reuse platform-watermarked exports.
- Retain license/source evidence with the project.

### Audio and export control

- Normalize clean speech consistently; target approximately `-14 LUFS` integrated for the finished short and peaks below `-1 dBTP` as an operating default.
- Remove severe noise; avoid robotic over-processing.
- Export clean master: H.264 MP4, 1080x1920, 30 fps, AAC 48 kHz.
- Watch every export on a phone with sound on and off.
- Preview inside each platform before posting because UI safe areas and crop behavior vary.

### Duration control

Cut in this order when a clip exceeds 45 seconds:

1. repeated examples;
2. transition phrases;
3. redundant product explanation;
4. B-roll holds;
5. end-card duration.

Do not cut the pain recognition, core reframe, correction boundary where needed, or CTA merely to reach an arbitrary number.

### Edit QA checklist

- [ ] Hook begins on frame one and matches the overlay.
- [ ] One pain moment; no second family entered.
- [ ] Track is explicit: `PS` tests a pain state; `CC` tests a category belief. The edit does not collapse both jobs.
- [ ] Brand appears after recognition.
- [ ] No architecture jargon or generic AI language.
- [ ] Category Contrast declares `conflict_intensity`; any direct attack turns from category conflict to Operational Reality.
- [ ] C3/C4 has an explicit G-04 conflict review covering truth, defensibility, target relevance, factual allegation, and brand risk.
- [ ] Category-level agency criticism does not identify or harass a specific company or person.
- [ ] The asset does not imply all automation is bad or that every automation provider skips understanding.
- [ ] No unsupported product, integration, outcome, or availability claim.
- [ ] Captions are verbatim, readable, and safe.
- [ ] Voice is clear on phone speakers.
- [ ] CTA says `SEE` and the platform’s keyword-to-DM path has a current QA receipt.
- [ ] Clip is 25–45 seconds.
- [ ] Clip ID, edit version, CTA code, and approval are embedded in records.

## H. Asset taxonomy and naming convention

### Stable dimensions

| Dimension | Codes |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign | `F100` |
| Wave | `W1`, `W2`, etc. |

| Experiment | `BASE`, `DIRECT_ATTACK_TEST`, `MESSAGE_CARD_LANGUAGE_TEST` |
| Conflict intensity | `C0`, `C1`, `C2`, `C3`, `C4` |
| Territory language | `PLAIN_FIRST_WITH_NAMED_BRIDGE`, `PLAIN_ONLY`, `NAMED_FIRST` |
| Avatar | `HR`, `CIMH`, `FF`, `BH`, `LP`, `HIRE`, `TSB`, `VAC`, `SC`, `TK`, `RP`, `CCU`, `CDT`, `OWN`, `TVB`, `MISS`, `AUTO`, `LEAD` |
| Pain family | `OD`, `VIS`, `FLOW`, `CAP`, `MIS` |
| Category Contrast family | `FEED`, `BEFORE_AUTOMATE`, `FASTER_WRONG`, `PROCESS_MISMATCH`, `MISSING_CONTEXT`, `CHANGE_IMPACT`, `HUMAN_JUDGMENT`, `AUTOMATION_READINESS`, `MISSING_LAYER` |

### Canonical clip ID

The pain-state clip ID describes the idea and never changes across platforms:

```text
{CAMPAIGN}-{WAVE}-{AVATAR}-{FAMILY}-{HOOK}-{ANGLE}
```

Example:

```text
F100-W1-HR-OD-PROV-A01
```

Category Contrast uses a separate identity so it cannot be mistaken for a
pain-state avatar:

```text
F100-CC-{FAMILY}-{NN}
```

Example:

```text
F100-CC-PROCESS_MISMATCH-02
```

The matched Direct Attack Test identifies the conflict level in the clip ID:

```text
F100-CC-DA-{C1|C2|C3|C4}-{NN}
```

Example: `F100-CC-DA-C4-01`.

### Script ID

```text
F100-W1-HR-OD-PROV-A01-S01
```

```text
F100_B01_L1_HR_A01_T01_CAMA_20260828.MOV
```

### Master and platform export

```text
F100-W1-HR-OD-PROV-A01_S01_MASTER_R01_20260830.mp4
F100-W1-HR-OD-PROV-A01_S01_IG-REEL_R01_CTA1_20260831.mp4
F100-W1-HR-OD-PROV-A01_S01_LI-FEED_R02_CTA3_20260901.mp4
```

### Post instance ID

One exported asset can be posted more than once. Each post receives an immutable instance ID:

```text
{CLIP-ID}-{CHANNEL}-{PLACEMENT}-P{NN}-{YYYYMMDD}
```

Example:

```text
F100-W1-HR-OD-PROV-A01-IG-REEL-P01-20260831
```

The dashboard uses post instance ID as its row key and clip ID as the grouping key.

Value bridge is metadata, not a replacement for the stable clip ID. It records what consequence the clip is testing after recognition: visibility, correction, advisor context, continuity, or a consequential decision.

### UTM convention

```text
utm_source={channel}
utm_medium={organic_social|paid_social|founder_outbound|email|referral}
utm_campaign=founding_100
utm_content={clip_id}_{edit}_{cta}
utm_term={pain_family}_{avatar}
```

Example:

```text
/founding100/quick?clip=F100-W1-HR-OD-PROV-A01&track=PS&hf=OD&pi={POST_INSTANCE_ID}&utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=dm&utm_campaign=founding_100&utm_content=F100-W1-HR-OD-PROV-A01_R01_CTA1
```

### Version rules

- Increment `S` when spoken claims, examples, reframe, or CTA wording changes.
- Increment `R` when pacing, captions, crop, B-roll, hook text, music, or end treatment changes.
- Do not reuse an old filename after replacing an export.
- Record every released filename, platform URL, publish time, and UTM in the dashboard.
- Archive a superseded master; do not delete or overwrite it.
