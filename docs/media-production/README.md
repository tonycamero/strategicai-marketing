## Start here

This folder turns the Founding 100 production brief into an operator-ready short-form media system. The plan is ready for scripting, filming, editing, tagging, and measurement. Public release remains gated by the decisions in `00_RELEASE_AUTHORITY_AND_DECISIONS.md`.

The 2026-08-25 Product Shift Authority Record ratifies the product hierarchy in
this folder. The Market Learning Charter separately authorizes controlled pain,
category, contrast, Message Card, and intended-experience tests.

Commercial gates do not automatically block a market-learning asset that makes
no purchase promise and leads only to a truthful, reviewed content destination.
Runtime claims, the `$299` offer, guaranteed deliverables, checkout, activation,
and the 90-day Nemo commitment remain evidence- and authority-gated.

The system has one job:

> Make the right owner recognize a painful business moment, reconsider what is causing it, want to see what StrategicAI would discover, and imagine the value of having Nemo reason with them from that company-specific understanding.

It now uses three complementary creative tracks:

- **Pain-state recognition (`PS`):** “That happened in my business.”
- **Category Contrast (`CC`):** “Maybe automation is not the first question.”
- **Message Cards (`MC`):** controlled language tests that ask which sentence earns `SEE`.

All three use the same active market-learning front door:

```text
MESSAGE / MEDIA
    -> COMMENT SEE
    -> DM: PICK YOUR DEPTH
       -> 5-MINUTE VIDEO
       -> 35-MINUTE WALKTHROUGH

OPTIONAL COMMERCIAL BRANCH, INACTIVE UNTIL SEPARATELY AUTHORIZED
    -> FOUNDING 100 OFFER
    -> VERIFIED CHECKOUT
    -> AUTHORIZED ACTIVATION
```

There is no email or identity gate before either content choice. The governing
route, attribution, event, abandonment, and implementation contract is
`14_COMMENT_SEE_FUNNEL_AND_MESSAGE_CARD_CONTRACT.md`. All three website-owned
pre-purchase routes are implemented with browser-tab attribution, an optional
analytics-only SEE reason, and honest YouTube/checkout configuration gates. The
DM transport remains owned by NemoClaw Social DM Automation; both video assets,
the approved Stripe destination and verification, legal routes, and the product
handoff remain unresolved as recorded there.

The primary contrast is **automation without understanding**. The positive
territory is **Operational Reality—how the business actually works**. Both
tracks converge on:

```text
I want to understand how my business actually works.
    -> Is “Operational Reality” the clearest name for that?
    -> I want to see what StrategicAI would discover.
    -> I already know what I would ask Nemo.
```

The canonical category hierarchy is:

```text
StrategicAI = Operational Intelligence built from Operational Reality
Business Views = the visible, inspectable expression
Nemo = the interaction layer
Automation / capabilities = downstream action when warranted
```

“An AI that knows your business” is optional plain-English shorthand, not the
primary company promise or product category.

StrategicAI is understanding-first, not anti-automation. Automation, agents,
process change, alerts, ownership, system connection, human judgment, or no
change may follow when the evidence warrants them.

For TOFU acquisition, **conflict is valid**. Category Contrast may directly
criticize AI automation agencies and automation-first offers from
`C0_NEUTRAL` through `C4_MAX_CONFLICT`. The attack targets the premise,
sequence, category behavior, or buying mistake. Named-company attacks,
personal attacks, fabricated harm, and unsupported factual allegations remain
prohibited. Strong automation builders remain possible partners.

The proposed commercial story, still gated, would continue beyond the picture:

```text
StrategicAI learns the business
    -> Business Views make the understanding inspectable and correctable
    -> Nemo works from that context
    -> the owner brings Nemo serious business questions for 90 days
    -> context deepens through use and correction
    -> the owner decides whether this kind of business intelligence is worth continuing
```

Business Views remain proposed inspection surfaces and intended inputs to the
context Nemo reasons from. They are not current guaranteed deliverables or the
hero product by themselves.

## Non-negotiable operating sequence

1. Clear the release gates; do not let editing begin before script claims are approved.
2. Prepare and film all eight Wave 1 clips in one concentrated batch.
3. Publish all eight before declaring a pain state a winner or loser.
4. Capture the matched C1–C4 Direct Attack Test in B02 during Week 2; C3/C4 require explicit G-04 conflict review.
5. Publish C1 and nuclear C4 in Week 2, then C2/C3 in early Week 3 under matched body, CTA, setting, and distribution controls.
6. Read the active funnel from `SEE` through content and qualified conversation;
   add paid-tenant measurement only after the commercial branch is authorized.
   Keep `PS`, `CC`, and `MC` separate and distinguish qualified-owner response
   from competitor reaction.
7. Expand only the pain states, category angles, or conflict levels that produce qualified owner behavior.
8. Feed market language back into scripts, 5-minute content, the 35-minute
   walkthrough, and product discovery. Feed it into an offer only under separate
   Commercial Commitment review.

## Definition of done for Wave 1

Wave 1 is complete when:

- eight approved masters and their platform variants are exported;
- every asset carries a valid clip or Message Card ID and governed attribution mapping;
- every platform post is entered in the dashboard;
- all comments, DMs, objections, and owner-language signals are logged;
- a seven-day read exists for every clip;
- the team records one decision per pain state: `EXPAND`, `ITERATE_HOOK`, `ITERATE_BODY`, `HOLD`, or `KILL_FOR_NOW`;
- question-intent, advisor-resonance, and expectation-risk signals are logged separately from ordinary engagement;
- the next production batch is selected from evidence, not preference.
