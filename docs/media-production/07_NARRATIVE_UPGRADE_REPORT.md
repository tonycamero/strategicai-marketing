## A. Narrative adjudication

### KEEP

- The earlier decision to keep **An AI that knows your business.** as the primary promise is superseded. The canonical category is **Operational Intelligence built from Operational Reality**. The former line remains optional plain-English shorthand only.
- Pain moments, the 18 recognition-state avatars, five pain families, and the one-pain-per-clip rule remain unchanged.
- `See it. Challenge it. Change it.`, the flashlight metaphor, correction as a feature, Business Views, founder-led production, one webinar CTA, and the full-funnel measurement chain remain.

### STRENGTHEN

- The commercial value now continues from company context to Nemo’s context-aware reasoning and the owner’s serious business questions.
- Business Views are framed as useful outputs, inspection/correction surfaces, evidence that understanding is being built, and inputs that improve the context Nemo reasons from.
- The desired prospect movement now ends with: **“I already know what I would ask Nemo.”**
- Measurement now captures advisor resonance and serious-question intent in addition to attention and conversion.

### REFRAME

- “Advisor” means a business-aware thinking partner working from accumulated, inspectable, correctable company context. It does not mean professional advice, omniscience, guaranteed correctness, automatic authority, or human replacement.
- Consultant comparison is about the time and durability of company understanding, never superior advice or accuracy.
- The value hierarchy is: learn the business → show it back → Nemo works from it → owner brings real questions → context deepens → owner decides whether to continue.

### GOVERN

The new claim-sensitive concepts are explicitly gated: business-aware advisor, AI advisor, built around your business, knows your business, retained/deep context, no start from zero, consultant comparison, and serious business questions. Prohibited claims are recorded in the release-control file.

### DEFER

The live `/founding100` surface has now been upgraded to carry the Business Intelligence Portfolio proposition and the two-layer value story. Webinar registration and sales/offer pages remain specified but unbuilt. Payment verification, Formation, Terms, Privacy, product architecture, integrations, and unrelated marketing files remain unresolved dependencies.

The stronger proposition is permissible as a bounded narrative hypothesis because it describes a context-building experience and intended way of working. It is not presented as a current universal capability, regulated advice service, guaranteed outcome, or autonomous authority.
