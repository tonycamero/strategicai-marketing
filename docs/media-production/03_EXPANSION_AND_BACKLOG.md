# Expansion Matrix and Concept Backlog

## CTA authority

`14_COMMENT_SEE_FUNNEL_AND_MESSAGE_CARD_CONTRACT.md` governs every executable
CTA in this backlog. The concept tables use the standard CTA as a draft control;
that does not approve any exact asset. Unless a declared CTA experiment is
separately reviewed, use:

```text
COMMENT_SEE_CTA = Comment SEE and I’ll send you the video.
```

The DM—not the post—offers the ungated 5-minute and 35-minute choices. Do not
silently convert a Message Card into a `PS` or `CC` video; `MC` remains a
separate language-testing track with its own manifest.

## Production gate

This file is a decision-ready roadmap, not an instruction to film everything. Wave 1 earns expansion by producing qualified owner behavior. A Tier 1 pain state should normally receive its next two angles only after it is marked `EXPAND` or `ITERATE_HOOK`. The third and fourth angles wait for confirming signal or a clear diagnostic need.

Recommended expansion order for a winning avatar:

1. Produce the angle that changes the hook mechanism while preserving the same pain.
2. Produce the angle that changes the specific moment while preserving the family.
3. Produce the angle that tests a different economic or emotional consequence.
4. Stop when marginal learning drops or delivery capacity becomes the constraint.

## Backlog activation rules

Activate a Tier 2 or Tier 3 avatar when at least one is true:

- the same pain appears in three or more qualified comments, DMs, calls, or outbound conversations in seven days;
- 5-minute/35-minute feedback or a `SEE` reason contains the pain more than once and the owner can describe a recent incident;
- an early tenant or applicant names the pain as a purchase driver;
- a Tier 1 clip creates adjacent comments that are more specific than its intended pain;
- founder-led outbound needs a matching follow-up asset for a recurring objection or moment.

Do not activate because the concept is personally interesting, easy to film, or likely to attract broad entertainment views.

## F. Category Contrast track — `CC`

recognition. Its job is to enter the active AI-agent and automation
conversation, challenge the starting assumption, introduce Operational

It must not be merged with pain-state screening or used to imply that
automation agencies are the enemy.
