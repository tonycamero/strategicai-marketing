# Release Authority and Decisions

## Release posture

The production system can be prepared, filmed, edited, and used for controlled
market learning. No asset should be published or promoted until the gates
applicable to that asset's truth layer, CTA, destination, and channel are
cleared.

The product hierarchy is now ratified. Publication review no longer asks
whether every experimental phrase is canonical before it can be tested. It asks
whether the phrase is a lawful, attributable market hypothesis and whether the
asset implies any unsupported runtime capability or commercial commitment.

This distinction prevents the prior commercial ladder from freezing market
learning while keeping unavailable delivery out of conversion copy.

## Authority resolution

```text
PRODUCT_DIRECTION = RATIFIED
MARKET_LEARNING = ACTIVE
FORMATION_VALIDATION = IN_PROGRESS
COMMERCIAL_DELIVERY = GATED
```

- Operational Reality -> Operational Intelligence is ratified product doctrine.
- Pain, category comprehension, contrast intensity, and value-bridge language
  remain market hypotheses.
- Business View generation and Nemo context behavior remain intended-product
  and runtime claims whose current wording must match evidence.
- The `$299` offer, included portfolio, 90-day delivery, checkout, and
  activation remain commercial commitments requiring separate authority.

## Locked strategy from the controlling brief

The following are treated as campaign instructions, not market facts:

- The campaign supports founder-led acquisition of the first 10 paid tenants and then 100 paid tenants within 90 days.
- Media supports outbound; it is not expected to acquire 100 customers by itself.
- Pain-state TOFU content begins with one pain moment; Category Contrast begins with one automation-market moment; Message Cards test one controlled sentence. All drive first to comment `SEE`.
- Wave 1 contains one clip for each of eight Tier 1 pain states.
- Category Contrast is a separate experimental track; it does not replace or renumber the pain-state slate.
- B02 is the matched C1–C4 Direct Attack Test; the softer/general Category Contrast slate remains backlog.
- Clips target 25–45 seconds, preferably 27–38 seconds.
- The creative sequence is pain or market moment, sharp reframe, intriguing possibility, `Comment SEE` CTA.
- Founder-led direct-to-camera is the default production style.
- Customer-facing names are StrategicAI and Nemo; `SAR` is prohibited.
- StrategicAI’s category is Operational Intelligence built from Operational Reality; “An AI that knows your business” is explanatory shorthand, not primary positioning.
- Measurement follows qualified attention through content and conversation now;
  paid-tenant progression becomes a valid scoreboard only when the commercial
  branch is authorized.
- Correction is part of the experience: an owner saying “No, that is not how it works” improves the picture.

## Ratified product hierarchy and proposed value bridge

```text
CANONICAL_CATEGORY = OPERATIONAL_INTELLIGENCE
FOUNDATIONAL_INPUT = OPERATIONAL_REALITY
VISIBLE_EXPRESSION = BUSINESS_VIEWS
INTERACTION_LAYER = NEMO
DOWNSTREAM_ACTION = AUTOMATION_AND_CAPABILITIES_WHEN_WARRANTED
PLAIN_ENGLISH_SHORTHAND = AN_AI_THAT_KNOWS_YOUR_BUSINESS
SHORTHAND_ROLE = EXPLANATORY_NOT_PRIMARY_POSITIONING
```

The campaign must carry the proposition in this order:

1. **StrategicAI is being built to form an evidence-bounded understanding of
   the organization’s Operational Reality.** The exact context available in a
   named experience remains a runtime claim.
2. **That understanding becomes Operational Intelligence.** It supports better questions and intervention decisions without implying omniscience or guaranteed outcomes.
3. **Business Views are intended to make bounded parts of the intelligence
   visible.** The owner can inspect, challenge, and correct the picture when a
   named view exists in the accepted experience.
4. **Nemo is intended to let the owner reason from lawfully available company
   context.** The exact context, continuity, and interaction behavior require
   runtime evidence.
5. **Automation and other capabilities may act from it when warranted.** They are downstream options, not the starting assumption or guaranteed scope.

The proposed 90-day Nemo continuation is Commercial Commitment architecture,
not part of the ratified product hierarchy and not an active purchase promise.
It may enter customer-facing offer copy only after G-01, G-07, G-08, and G-09
close with the required evidence and authority.

“An AI that knows your business” remains available as a plain-English bridge
for audiences who need the idea translated quickly. It must not be presented
as StrategicAI’s primary company promise, canonical category, or top-level
positioning.

The intended reaction begins in ordinary owner language—“I want to understand
how my business actually works”—before asking whether the named territory
`Operational Reality` earns comprehension. It then continues to:

> **What would I ask if my advisor already knew my business?**

## Canonical acquisition front door

`14_COMMENT_SEE_FUNNEL_AND_MESSAGE_CARD_CONTRACT.md` governs the acquisition
path and supersedes gated webinar registration as the primary front door:

```text
PS / CC / MC
    -> COMMENT SEE
    -> DM
    -> 5 MIN or 35 MIN, ungated content
    -> CONTINUE CONTENT or QUALIFIED CONVERSATION

OPTIONAL COMMERCIAL BRANCH, ONLY AFTER SEPARATE AUTHORITY
    -> FOUNDING 100 OFFER
    -> VERIFIED CHECKOUT
    -> AUTHORIZED ACTIVATION
```

No email, phone, company, registration, application, or account field may
stand between `SEE` and the selected content. Legacy direct-webinar and “link
below” CTAs in source material are not approved production copy. They must use
the canonical `Comment SEE and I’ll send you the video.` CTA unless a declared
CTA test is separately approved.

## Primary category contrast

The primary market contrast is:

> **Most AI automation offers begin by asking what can be automated. StrategicAI begins by understanding how the organization actually works.**

```text
PRIMARY_CATEGORY_CONTRAST = AUTOMATION_WITHOUT_UNDERSTANDING
POSITIVE_TERRITORY = OPERATIONAL_REALITY
TRANSFORMATION = OPERATIONAL_REALITY_TO_OPERATIONAL_INTELLIGENCE
CAMPAIGN_DISTINCTION = UNDERSTANDING_FIRST_NOT_AUTOMATION_FIRST
```

Operational Reality means how the organization actually works: people, work,
systems, information, handoffs, decisions, authority, dependencies,
constraints, exceptions, workarounds, unknowns, and competing perspectives.

The allowed campaign sequence is:

```text
Operational Reality
    -> understanding
    -> Operational Intelligence
    -> decision
    -> automation, agents, process change, alerts, ownership,
       simplification, investigation, human judgment, or no change
```

Automation is a possible downstream result. The campaign is not anti-automation,
and automation builders remain possible referral, implementation, capability,
or customer partners.

### Conflict is a valid acquisition tool

StrategicAI may directly and aggressively criticize AI automation agencies,
automation-first offers, and dominant agent-market assumptions in TOFU media.
The authorized target is the category premise, sequence, behavior, or buying
mistake. The purpose is to stop the right owner, challenge the default
assumption, earn qualified `SEE`, and move attention through content toward a
truthful next step. That step may become an offer only after commercial
authority exists.

```text
CONFLICT_INTENSITY =
C0_NEUTRAL
C1_CONTRARIAN
C2_DIRECT_ATTACK
C3_HARD_ATTACK
C4_MAX_CONFLICT
```

| Level | Authorized posture | Example territory | Review standard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `C0` | Educational contrast | Understand the workflow before automating it. | Ordinary G-04 claim review |
| `C1` | Challenges conventional wisdom | The market may be starting one step too late. | Ordinary G-04 claim review |
| `C2` | Direct category criticism | Most AI automation agencies are starting in the wrong place. | Claim defensibility and target relevance |
| `C3` | Hard attack on category behavior or buying logic | If the agency does not understand the company, it is automating a guess. | Explicit G-04 conflict review before publication |
| `C4` | Maximum truthful conflict | The AI automation gold rush is automating chaos. | Explicit G-04 conflict review before publication |

Provocation is not itself a defect. Review asks whether the claim is truthful,
defensible, relevant to qualified owners, free of unnecessary factual
allegation, and worth the brand risk. It does not require neutralizing an asset
merely because the asset may create disagreement.

### Category-contrast claim boundaries

| Phrase or idea | Allowed meaning | Required boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Automation without understanding | A category error in which implementation begins before the surrounding work, ownership, information, exceptions, and effects are visible enough to make a responsible decision. | Category-level criticism is authorized; do not invent misconduct, evidence, or universal failure. |
| Understand it before you automate it | Inspect the real work and its surrounding conditions before deciding whether and how to automate. | Do not claim StrategicAI must precede every automation or that automation is usually wrong. |
| Operational Reality | How the organization actually works, including differences between stated process and lived work. | Do not imply omniscient, complete, or real-time knowledge. |
| Operational Intelligence | A useful, evidence-bounded understanding of the operating business that supports better-scoped questions and decisions. | Do not imply diagnosis, certainty, automatic implementation, or guaranteed outcomes. |
| “Your feed is full of agents” / “everyone is selling agents” | Testable recognition language for owners who report frequent AI-agent and automation offers. | Treat prevalence as an assumption; avoid unsupported universal market claims. |
| Faster wrong | Speed does not correct a misunderstood handoff, decision, or process. | Do not claim automation itself causes failure or never improves a process. |
| Human judgment | Some decisions may require accountable human authority or exception handling. | Do not claim agents are inherently unsafe or ineffective. |
| Automation readiness | A bounded decision about whether the work is understood, stable, owned, and supported enough for a proposed automation. | Do not present readiness as an automatic recommendation or guarantee. |

Direct category attack is authorized. The following remain prohibited:

- naming and disparaging a specific agency, company, or creator without separate evidence and authority;
- personal attacks, harassment, or mockery of individual creators;
- false factual claims, fabricated failure rates, invented customer harm, accusations of fraud, or incompetence presented as fact;
- unsupported claims that AI automation does not work;
- claims that StrategicAI must precede every automation;
- blanket anti-automation messaging;
- claims that agents are inherently unsafe, ineffective, or incapable;
- claims that StrategicAI automatically implements every intervention it identifies;
- deceptive comparisons or unsupported claims that StrategicAI is superior to every agency;
- unsupported financial-loss claims;
- screenshots, stitches, or reaction-video formats that identify and target a specific competitor without separate authority.

### Advisor-claim boundaries

| Phrase or idea | Allowed meaning | Required boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business-aware advisor / AI advisor | Nemo can reason with the owner from accumulated, company-specific context in the approved experience. | Not licensed professional advice, guaranteed correctness, autonomous authority, or human replacement. |
| Built around your business | The context and questions are shaped by the owner’s company, people, systems, goals, constraints, corrections, and unresolved questions. | Not an immediate claim that Nemo knows the whole business inside-out. |
| Knows your business | A campaign promise for a context-building experience that grows through evidence and correction. | Never imply instant, complete, omniscient, or infallible knowledge. |
| Retains / remembers context | Prior context is available within a named, verified experience and scope. | Do not use until the actual retention boundary is verified. Prefer “works from the context you have built” when uncertain. |
| Serious business questions | Consequential questions the owner would normally bring to an operator, strategist, consultant, or trusted advisor. | This describes intended use and curiosity; it does not promise an answer, outcome, or professional judgment. |
| Consultant comparison | A human consultant must spend time learning the company; StrategicAI is intended to make company understanding a durable part of the product experience. | Never claim Nemo is better, more accurate, or universally superior to consultants. |
| No start from zero | The owner does not need to reconstruct the same approved company context for every conversation. | Use only where the context is actually available in a named, runtime-verified experience. |

Prohibited advisor claims include: “better than any consultant,” “more accurate than a consultant,” “never forgets,” “knows everything,” “always available,” professional legal/accounting/medical advice, guaranteed improved decisions, and human replacement.

## Governing narrative boundaries

Every customer-facing asset must preserve these rules:

- “An AI that knows your business” is optional explanatory shorthand for the context-building idea. It is not the primary company promise and must not imply instant, complete, or infallible truth.
- StrategicAI begins from organization context and people’s evidence; perspectives can differ and may need correction.
- Nemo may help the owner inspect and follow the picture. The copy must not imply autonomous management, diagnosis, external action, universal monitoring, or guaranteed outcomes.
- A possible Business View is not a guaranteed fixed deliverable unless the commercial offer expressly includes it.
- The campaign may target SMB owners without redefining StrategicAI as a product exclusively for SMBs.
- “First it knows your business. Then you give it jobs.” is directional. It does not promise that downstream jobs or integrations are included in the Founding 100 purchase.
- “Understand it before you automate it” challenges a starting assumption; it does not make StrategicAI an anti-automation brand.
- AI automation agencies may be named and criticized as a category. Specific people and companies may not be targeted without separate evidence and authority.
- The substantive enemy is automation without adequate understanding. Conflict intensity is an acquisition variable, not permission to fabricate misconduct.

| Asset or claim class | Required before publication | Not automatically required |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Ratified product doctrine | Correct tense and truth-layer review | Full Formation completion |
| Nemo intended-experience hypothesis | G-04/G-08 experiment review; no offer-page use as proven fact | Current runtime receipt only when the line is clearly a controlled TOFU hypothesis |

An asset that changes truth layer changes its applicable gates. Reusing a
market-tested sentence on an offer page is a new claim review, not a mechanical
copy reuse.

## Open decisions before the first edit lock

1. What exact city, radius, or definition makes the audience “local”?
2. Which comment-to-DM transport is authorized for each launch channel?
3. Where will the 5-minute and 35-minute assets be hosted, and which player exposes reliable progress events?
4. Which available content or conversation step should the 5-minute close
   prioritize now, and should the Founding 100 offer be considered later after
   Commercial Commitment authority?
5. Which Business Views are examples versus contractually included outputs?
6. Which channels already have a credible founder audience and profile history?
7. Is paid promotion authorized in Wave 1, or does paid begin only after organic signal?
8. What weekly delivery-capacity ceiling pauses media spend or publishing?
9. Who owns response coverage when the founder is unavailable?
10. What approved analytics destination receives funnel events, and what session attribution mechanism satisfies G-05?
11. Which exact context is available to Nemo throughout the 90-day experience, and what is not retained?
12. Which real owner decisions are in scope for the Founding 100 experience versus merely examples?
13. Which Category Contrast headline earns the strongest qualified `SEE` and content progression without overstating market prevalence?
14. Does the market understand “Operational Reality” immediately, or does “how the business actually works” need to lead?
15. Which automation-partner posture should be tested first: referral source, pre-build intelligence layer, or downstream implementation partner?
16. Which conflict intensity produces the strongest qualified-owner conversion rather than the most total engagement?
17. What explicit stop threshold should pause a C3/C4 asset for qualified-prospect brand risk?
