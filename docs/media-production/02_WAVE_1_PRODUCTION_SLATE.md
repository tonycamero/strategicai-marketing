## Slate rules

- `PAIN_STATE_ARCHITECTURE = PRESERVED`: these eight `PS` clips remain the complete Wave 1 pain-state slate.

- The C1–C4 Direct Attack Test is a separately governed `CC` experiment and is not Wave 1 clip 9–12.
- Record all eight before changing the strategy in response to one early post.
- Speak the exact hook on the first usable frame; do not precede it with a greeting or logo.
- The script is a delivery guide, not permission to sound read. Preserve the meaning and approved claim boundary if natural wording changes on set.
- Capture three complete takes and the standard CTA pickups defined in the shoot system.
- Keep the master clean. Platform logos, music, captions, and CTA behavior belong in platform variants.

## Clip 1 — You Became the Workflow

**Clip ID:** `F100-W1-HR-OD-PROV-A01`<br>
**Avatar:** Human Router<br>
**Pain family:** Owner Dependence<br>
**Hook type:** Provocation<br>
**Exact hook:** “You may have accidentally become one of your company’s business processes.”

### Production draft script

> You may have accidentally become one of your company’s business processes.
>
> If every decision comes to you, every handoff waits, and everyone asks what happens next, you are not just managing the workflow. You are inside it.
>
> StrategicAI builds a picture of who needs what from whom and where work stops. Inspect and correct it. Then bring Nemo the question: “What is still too dependent on me?” Nemo has company context to start from.
>
> Comment SEE and I’ll send you the video.

### Production card

| Field | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Environment | Founder desk with active notebook, ordinary office, no staged tech wall |
| Framing | Medium close-up, eye-level, shoulders visible; 9:16 center-safe |
| Delivery | Calm provocation; slight pause after “business processes” and “inside it” |
| Opening text | `YOU BECAME THE WORKFLOW` |
| Supporting text | At the reframe only: `WHAT WOULD YOU ASK?` |
| B-roll | Optional 1-second inserts: unread messages, decision queue, hand pointing between whiteboard boxes |
| CTA | “Comment SEE and I’ll send you the video.” |
| Target length | 34–38 seconds |
| Value bridge | `ADVISOR` — owner dependence becomes a serious question for Nemo after the picture is built. |
| Advisor layer | Explicit |
| Reason for test | The phrase turns familiar owner dependence into a new mental picture, then shows why company context changes the question the owner can bring to Nemo. |
| Hypothesis | Qualified owners will repeat “I became the workflow” and name a real owner-dependence question, producing both recognition and `INT-QUESTION` signal. |

## Clip 2 — The Bus Test

**Clip ID:** `F100-W1-CIMH-OD-TEST-A01`<br>
**Avatar:** Company-in-My-Head<br>
**Pain family:** Owner Dependence<br>
**Hook type:** Test<br>
**Exact hook:** “Want to know whether your business lives in your head?”

### Production draft script

> Want to know whether your business lives in your head?
>
> Ask what would disappear if you could not answer the phone for thirty days: the exceptions, promises, customer history, and rules only you know.
>
> That is not a documentation problem by itself. It is a company-memory problem.
>
> StrategicAI builds a working picture your people can challenge and correct. Then ask Nemo, “What would break if I disappeared?” The point is not perfect memory. It is not rebuilding the company story from zero.
>
> Comment SEE and I’ll send you the video.

| Framing | Medium shot; founder steps toward camera on the opening question |
| Delivery | Reflective, not ominous; list the exceptions as remembered examples |
| Opening text | `DOES THE BUSINESS LIVE IN YOUR HEAD?` |
| Supporting text | `CONTEXT BEFORE ADVICE` |
| B-roll | Founder closing laptop, unanswered phone on desk, handwritten exception note |
| CTA | “Comment SEE and I’ll send you the video.” |
| Target length | 36–40 seconds |
| Value bridge | `ADVISOR+CONTINUITY` — company memory becomes context for a real absence question. |
| Advisor layer | Explicit |
| Reason for test | An owner can perform the test immediately, recognize unwritten context, and imagine bringing the resulting question to an advisor. |
| Hypothesis | The concrete 30-day absence scenario will create saves, “this is us” comments, and serious questions about what the company context could support. |

### What changed across Wave 1

- The hooks and pain moments remain intact.
- Five clips now make the advisor consequence explicit; three leave it implicit so the slate still feels like recognition media rather than eight product ads.
- Every bridge now answers why company context matters to the next question, not only why a map is useful.
- No clip promises that Nemo is omniscient, professionally licensed, always correct, universally available, or superior to a human advisor.
- All eight preserve the single canonical `SEE` CTA.

- [ ] Correction is visible or at least not contradicted.
- [ ] One `Comment SEE` CTA is used.
- [ ] The channel has a verified keyword-to-DM transport before publication.
