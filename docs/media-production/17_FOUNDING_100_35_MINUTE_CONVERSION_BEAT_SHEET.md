# Founding 100 35-Minute Conversion Beat Sheet

## Document contract

This is a roughly 35-minute conversion argument for a qualified owner. It is
not final narration, slide copy, page copy, a product specification, or a
commercial contract.

```text
TARGET_TOTAL = 35:00
HARD_MAXIMUM = 35:00
ASSUME_LAUNCH_STATE_PROOF = TRUE_FOR_PLANNING
FINAL_NARRATION = NOT_AUTHORIZED
APPLICATION_SOURCE_CHANGES = 0
```

The launch assumption means the finished presentation is designed to show real,
accepted, demonstrable proof of the named product behaviors. It does not mean
the current repository already contains every proof receipt. Pre-publication
release review must verify every assumed product/runtime asset. Customer
outcomes, testimonials, case studies, ROI, and public artifacts remain subject
to separate evidence, consent, and approval.

## Launch proof posture

The proof burden in the main argument is deliberately carried by product
demonstration at launch:

```text
EVIDENCE / PEOPLE / SYSTEMS / WORK
    -> FORMED UNDERSTANDING
    -> DISAGREEMENT + UNKNOWN PRESERVATION
    -> CORRECTION
    -> OPERATIONAL REALITY
    -> OPERATIONAL INTELLIGENCE
    -> BUSINESS VIEWS
    -> NEMO QUESTIONS
    -> WARRANTED ACTION
```

The presentation should show, with a real accepted capture:

- Formation forming a bounded picture from real organizational evidence;
- multiple perspectives or disagreement remaining visible;
- an explicit unknown remaining unknown;
- an owner correction changing the accepted picture;
- at least one useful bounded Business View;
- Nemo reasoning from accepted company context; and
- one consequential owner question following relationships or dependencies.

The finished asset should not present a mock UI as production, a storyboard as
a customer result, or a process diagram as proof that the runtime already did
the work. Process proof is used to orient the viewer; accepted product proof
does the demonstration work.

## Commercial branch rule

The final six minutes are designed for the founder-approved Founding 100
strategy, but remain non-executable until G-01, G-07, G-09, runtime, legal,
payment, and delivery inputs close.

When the commercial branch is inactive, the presentation ends after the
desired-future/qualified-next-step beat. It does not show price or ask for a
purchase. When active, it uses the exact authorized scope and terms and keeps
the same calm, explicit risk posture.

## Modular continuation openings

The first 60–120 seconds changes according to the acquisition state, then
converges at BEAT-02. These are opening variants, not five separate webinars.

| SOURCE | OPENING VARIANT | BELIEF TO HAND OFF | DO NOT REPEAT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PS pain-state recognition | Start with the exact owner moment that earned the click: recurring interruption, owner dependence, broken handoff, late awareness, or unresolved problem. | B01: the moment may be a signal, not an isolated incident. | The full pain-state clip or a generic company introduction. |
| CC category contrast | Start with the automation premise: “If the system does not understand how the company works, it is automating a guess.” Acknowledge that automation may still be right. | B07: understanding precedes intervention choice. | Repeating the attack or making the webinar a competitor rant. |
| MC Message Card | Re-open the exact sentence or tension that earned `SEE`, then ask what would have to be true for it to matter in the owner’s business. | The next belief implied by the card, usually B02, B04, or B07. | Treating the card as proof or replaying the same hook without advancement. |
| FOUNDER_OUTBOUND | Begin with the observed or reported operating concern that prompted the referral or outreach. | B02 or B12, depending on whether consequence or fit is already established. | Broad TOFU exposition or pretending the founder knows more than was shared. |
| REFERRAL | Acknowledge the referral context, then move immediately to the owner’s own operating reality. | B04: how the business actually works is the object of inquiry. | Borrowed social proof or unverified claims about the referrer. |
